#Clinical Research Context
Clinical trial registries contain information about studies including:
- Study conditions
- Interventions
- Recruitment status
- Study phase
- Enrollment
- Sponsors
- Study locations
- Primary and secondary outcomes
Projects like this can help researchers organize trial information and answer questions about the clinical research landscape.

#Example Questions
This project can be used to explore questions such as:
- How many trials are recruiting for a particular condition?
- What phases of clinical trials are most common?
- Which organizations sponsor the most studies?
- How does trial enrollment vary across studies?
- What proportion of studies are completed, recruiting, or terminated?
- How are clinical trials distributed across different disease areas?
